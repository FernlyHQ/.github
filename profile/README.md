# Fernly 👋🏻

**Ideas grow here.**

Fernly is a small technology company from Aotearoa New Zealand, creating software, custom hardware, SaaS digital products and automation that make work simpler.

We build our own products, solve interesting technical problems, and occasionally work alongside other businesses to turn good ideas into useful software.

## What we build

Our work spans:

- **Software products** : focused SaaS tools designed to solve specific problems well
- **Automation** : removing repetitive work and connecting systems intelligently
- **Applications** : practical, reliable software for real-world use
- **Integrations** : making existing platforms and data work better together
- **Prototypes & experiments** : exploring ideas quickly and learning what is worth building
- **Custom Hardware** : Custom-built and developed hardware for businesses.

We like small, focused products over unnecessarily complicated platforms.

- MedTech

- Manufacturing & Operations
- Startups & Emerging Products

## How we work

A few principles guide what we build:

**Keep it useful**  
Technology should solve a real problem, not create another one.

**Keep it simple**  
Simple software is easier to understand, maintain and improve.

**Build thoughtfully**  
Good engineering decisions matter, even when nobody sees them.

**Automate the boring stuff**  
People should spend more time doing valuable work and less time moving information between systems.

**Ship, learn, improve**  
Useful software gets better through real-world use.

## How we build

Fernly is built around an experienced New Zealand development team with real-world experience across software, technology and the industries we work in.

We don't just build from a specification. We bring years of experience, lessons learned, industry knowledge and an understanding of how technology has evolved to every project. We look at what has worked before, where technology is heading, and what makes sense for the business today.

**We write code.**

We use AI as a powerful development tool, but never as a replacement for experience, engineering judgement or quality. AI can help us move faster, explore ideas and improve productivity, but the responsibility for what we build remains with us.

We understand the code we deliver, how it works, why it was designed that way, and the infrastructure it runs on.

Our software and hardware projects follow structured development processes covering architecture, design, development, testing, deployment and ongoing maintainability. We build with quality, security, reliability and future growth in mind.

When working with clients and external teams, we work collaboratively and transparently. Projects are broken into clear stages with regular milestones, working deliverables and opportunities for feedback throughout the process.

We aren't interested in churning out disposable software as quickly as possible.

We want to build technology that lasts.

That means combining modern tools, automation and AI with proven engineering practices, practical experience and a healthy amount of old-school technical knowledge.

**Modern technology. Experienced people. Thoughtful engineering.**
